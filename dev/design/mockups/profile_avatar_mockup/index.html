1 · Four directions, same three subjects

Same cat / kid / ghost drawn four ways, so the comparison is fair. Pick a direction and I'll build all ten in it.

A · Flat GeoPure flat fills, zero outlines. Netflix-ish. Reads cleanest when small.
B · Sticker OutlineHeavy dark keyline around every shape. Most punch, survives busy backdrops.
C · MonolineOne accent stroke on near-black. Most "premium", least playful. Fits the dark UI.
D · Layered PaperFlat + one darker tone for depth. Warmer, still no gradients.
