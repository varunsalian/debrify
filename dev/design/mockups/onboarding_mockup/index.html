First Run

The shipped flow is a 560px dialog on a slate gradient, sitting inside a scroll view, with a chip wall, a one-line text field and a keyboard that lands on top of it. This replaces it with a full-screen, two-column stage in the same idiom as Spotlight and Showcase: neutral #1B1C1C ground, SF stack, borderless focus that lifts rather than rings, and a standing rail on the left that tells you where you are the entire way through.

Three structural fixes carry most of the value: the keyboard can never cover the field (they are two fixed bands, not a stack), every step is reversible and skippable, and the phone is a first-class input device for the one thing a remote is terrible at — typing a 40-character token.

01 · TELEVISION — 1920 × 1080The flow, playable

Click the frame, then drive it: arrows move focus, Enter activates, Backspace goes back. LEFT from the first column parks on the Back button — the same "left edge is the way out" grammar the TV sidebar uses. Nothing here is geometric traversal; every move is an explicit target.

02 · THE KEYBOARD FIXTwo bands, never a stack

Today the API-key field is one line inside a SingleChildScrollView in a 560-wide dialog; the TV in-app keyboard opens over the bottom of the screen and the field is behind it, so on a couch you are typing a 40-character token you cannot see. The redesign makes the geometry structural rather than incidental:

BandOccupiesRule
Railx 42–125 (logical)Back only. Collapses on this step — the task owns the screen.
Field bandy 48–283Header, method tabs, the token echo and its live status. Fixed.
Keyboard bandy 314–540Keys + the action column. Fixed. It cannot overlap the field because it is a sibling with its own height, not an overlay.

The echo itself is readable from three metres: 19px logical mono, 2.5px tracking, grouped in fours, with a live caret. Status sits directly under it and speaks in one line — Nothing entered yet → 32 of 40 → Checking… → Premium · 245 days left. There is no error text that appears only after you commit.

Three ways in, not one. Typing is the fallback, not the expectation. Send from my phone reuses the remote-control channel that already exists (RemoteCommandRouter) for a single key rather than a whole config, and Paste is offered up front because most users open the token page on a phone and copy it. On phone/desktop the same three appear as a segmented control.

03 · IMPORTA checklist that stays put

The shipped guide auto-advances five mock phone screens every five seconds, so the instruction you are reading moves out from under you. Here the three steps are a static checklist that ticks as the transfer progresses, and the right panel carries the live half: this device's advertised name in large type, the connection state, and each item as it lands. A dead transfer shows "Lost the phone — reconnect and it resumes" instead of silently pulsing forever.

04 · PHONE — 360 × 740 LOGICALSame flow, thumb-first

One column, segmented progress at the top, and a CTA that is sticky above the keyboard rather than below the fold. The key screen is the same two-band idea expressed for a soft keyboard: the field is pinned directly above the inset, the CTA rides on top of it, and a clipboard match is offered as a chip instead of being buried in a menu.

07 · DPADExplicit targets, no geometric traversal

Each screen declares its own grid. Movement is clamped, never wrapped — an unreachable focus is worse on a remote than a dead end. LEFT at column 0 is the single exception: it parks on Back, and RIGHT returns you exactly where you were.

Focus survives the step change. Every transition targets a named node in a post-frame callback, the same pattern _requestFocusForCurrentStep already uses — but it is declared per screen in one table rather than reconstructed from _stepIndex arithmetic.
Not built here, on purpose. The spring focus engine (ζ≈0.82 overshoot) is a CSS approximation in this mock; in Flutter it is the existing FocusExpression mechanic from the Spotlight look, reused rather than reinvented. Same for the ident/splash handoff — unchanged.
