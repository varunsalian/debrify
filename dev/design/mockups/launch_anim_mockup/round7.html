Boot idents — v2 concepts

Five fresh directions, built from scratch. Each is ~2.4s of motion plus a 0.5s fade-to-app, sized in a 960×540 design space so it scales identically on phone, desktop and 10-foot TV. Click any stage to replay; use the buttons for fullscreen and slow-motion.

Aperture

01

A single hairline ring draws itself, blooms outward past frame, and its passing opens an iris wipe that reveals the wordmark as the letter-spacing settles. Quiet, optical, camera-shutter confidence.

Cheap on TVClipRect + stroke tween No blur

Trace

02

One point of light runs the width of the frame drawing a rule beneath the name; letters resolve in its wake, then the rule retracts to a centred tick. Reads as a signal locking on — on brand for a streaming app.

Cheap on TVPer-letter blur (drop on low-end) Translate + dash tween

Chroma

03

Split colour channels drift in from either side, converge into one perfectly sharp wordmark and release a soft flash. The most "studio ident" of the set — the moment of registration is the whole idea.

3 layers + screen blendBlur tween Colourable per theme
D

Fold

04

Three panels hinge in on the Y axis to build a solid monogram, a specular sweep crosses it, then the mark slides left and the wordmark unmasks behind it into a proper lockup. Ends on a reusable app-icon shape.

No blur3D rotate (Transform) Doubles as app icon

Bloom

05

A seed of light ignites, two rings pulse outward, and the letters rise centre-out as each ring passes through them. Warmest of the five and by far the cheapest to run — the safe default for weak Android TV boxes.

Cheapest on TVScale + opacity only Optional letter blur
