round 4 — five new materials

Rounds 2 & 3 already own aurora glass, gilded serif, black-hole starlight, neon, chrome, hairline restraint, lens iris, construction linework, water, iron, paper, anamorphic streak, star chart, satin and bokeh. This round opens five unclaimed materials — a corona of rim light, hologram diffraction, dust condensing into type, interference fringes, and cathedral god-rays. Each still lands on a static final frame.

Feasibility. Everything here is 2-D vector paint — gradients, clips, and at most one bounded mask layer (Hologram and Moiré reuse the offscreen + source-atop/source-in trick Prism already ships in Flutter). No shaders required on TV, no assets to decode before first frame. Halo's corona is a stroke-with-glow plus inner fill (no mask); Cathedral is six static rotated gradient wedges (8 draws); Motes is ~140 dots (halve to ~70 on TV, drawn batched) with all motion progress-derived so the settled frame is motionless. Glows use the two-stroke idiom already shipped. The ~300 ms native window before Flutter's first frame stays a static colour for all five.
