Settings in this concept

Unchanged two-pane, plus one guide-specific pane: Guide — day span (1/3/7 days cached), grid density, “start on Guide vs start on last channel”, and the per-playlist EPG source moved here from the source rows (it’s a guide concern, not a source concern).

Why this concept: planning and recording become the page’s native verbs. Everyone’s parents already know how to use it.

Watch out: the day-schedule endpoint is per-channel — a visible grid of 8 rows needs 8 cached schedules. Your 30-min schedule cache + lazy row binding covers it, but the +12h jump must not fan out 50 fetches; it pages like the channel list does.
