Concept 5 · DVR Hub

Recording becomes the identity: IPTV reorganizes into four destinations — Watch · Guide · Library · Scheduled. Library is the in-app recordings browser the engine has been missing (play, resume bars, NEW badges, delete, storage meter); Scheduled is a real agenda with countdowns and conflict warnings. Watch and Guide carry the live/browse duties (Concept 1’s shelves and Concept 2’s grid slot straight in).

Settings in this concept

The Recording section GROWS into the hub’s config: default recording quality-of-life (end padding on/off for manual timers, concurrent cap display, storage location + meter) — and everything navigational (scheduled list, library) moves OUT of settings onto the tabs where it belongs. Sources/ lists/startup keep the two-pane.

Why this concept: you just built a DVR nobody can see. This makes it the headline — and Library is the missing piece users will ask for the day recording ships.

Watch out: four tabs is a bigger navigation change than the others; zap-first users must still get to live TV in one press (Watch tab = startup-channel behavior preserved).
