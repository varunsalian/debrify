## Ordered session

1. **Prepare provenance and samples.** Record the physical device/Android version, final SHA, APK hash and relevant settings. Prepare a series with multiple seasons and missing episodes, a movie collection, debrid/torrent/direct/PikPak/protected sources, addon/external subtitles, a Stremio TV lineup, and IPTV live/archive/VOD entries with category pages, logos and missing artwork. Include failing/slow sources, a provider supporting strict TS and an Xtream HLS channel needing its `.ts` twin. Mark unavailable provider/hardware scenarios pending with the reason.
2. **VOD starts and late metadata (p2, p18, p19).** Start a movie and a series episode from each source type: debrid, torrent, direct, PikPak and a protected stream requiring MIME discovery. Verify playback, header-dependent access, buffering behavior and saved-position resume. On a supported hard codec, verify decoder fallback. Let episode metadata arrive after launch: title/identity, guide episodes and progress update correctly.
3. **Seven controls skins (p13, p15, p16).** Select CLASSIC, OTT, FROST, MARQUEE, BROADCAST, PULSE and TICKET in turn and start a fresh Activity each time (settings are read at launch). In each: inspect dock buttons/captions/focus order, title/OTT identity; show, hide and auto-hide while playing and paused; play/pause from dock and remote; cinema scrub hold/release and cancel; open/close the playlist during playback and navigate its edges/long lists with DPAD. Verify the simple title returns on the next reveal. Cycle aspect including 4:3, speed with audio pitch, night-mode loudness on/off and across track/audio-route changes, system audio effects, and refresh-rate/display-mode matching on supported hardware. Include IPTV live-dock styling.
4. **Series, movie playlists and episode protocol (p1, p2, p3, p10, p13, p16, p18).** Check series season tabs, episode rows, current highlight, now-playing, watched/progress and missing-episode rows; check the movie collection playlist. Verify next/previous and player-side episode fetch, catalog controls, quick-play next-episode hand-back, end-of-item auto-advance and the Next overlay. Near intro/credits, offer and activate Skip; near the end, check Up Next both auto-advance and dismiss. Exercise show shuffle start, continuation and exhaustion; judge valid eligible picks and no unwanted repetition, rather than demanding a particular random episode.
5. **Sources, startup gate and retry UI (p3, p8, p9, p10).** For movie and series, start with dead first sources: automatic failover commits after first frame. Pick a source before and after first frame, including a failing pick/restore; keep resume position. Exhaust every source and verify finish/report hand-back. Verify automatic recovery after a dead link, both source badge refresh paths and status-pill messages. Switch during shuffle. Test a PikPak cold-storage switch/start, retry backoff, reactivation indicator and exhaustion; then fetch more sources after all initial sources fail and inspect both browser/menu loading and result refreshes.
6. **Subtitles and auto-sync (p1, p2, p3, p4, p14).** Fetch addon subtitles for a movie and an episode, auto-select and retry a failed addon; inspect menu/track labels and subtitle identity/query rows. Use the search dialog including season/episode prompt and manual identity correction. Load external subtitles and verify timing/offset, switching tracks including Off, settings-panel refresh and loading/status pills. Run manual auto-sync and automatic verify, watching the ring animation and all auto-sync pill states. Include a long subtitle file on a low-RAM box; mark that hardware case pending if unavailable.
7. **IPTV boot, pipeline and live recovery (p2, p5, p11, p15, p17, p19).** Launch a payload carrying sources, lists and categories; restore the startup/remembered channel, including a Stremio IPTV channel with failing first candidates. Play HLS, TS, the Xtream `.ts` twin and strict-TS providers; inspect buffering/header-dependent playback and live-edge/retry behavior on a flaky source. Verify IPTV live dock styling. Stall a channel and inspect the reconnect pill; drop/restore Wi-Fi, then background with Home and resume live TV.
8. **Four IPTV guide styles and zapping (p1, p2, p6, p11, p12).** Relaunch separately with CLASSIC, GLASS, EDITION and CONSOLE. Check overlay layout/fonts/tokens, open/close, category browse/search, category/list pickers, favorites toggle and removing a row from a custom list. Check the number-jump dialog. Zap up/down and by number across category pages with the guide both open and closed; verify banner logos, now/next, LIVE/REC badges, favorites-gated jump hint and floating/docked behavior. Verify now/next header with a poster/logo and uppercase letter fallback. Exercise slow-provider EPG load including the 45-second timeout path. Include IPTV VOD episode next/previous.
9. **Stremio TV guide (p1, p2, p5, p7, p9, p18, p19).** Check guide data, opening/closing, search and channel switches including a failing channel. Verify startup with failing candidates, next-item/auto-advance, guide closure once playback is ready, and Back inside both list and search box. Inspect late channel/episode metadata.
10. **Archive, EPG and recording (p2, p3, p5, p11, p12).** Open the EPG schedule pane, navigate days and exercise replay/record actions. Use Start Over/catch-up on an archive-enabled channel, including after a channel switch. Start/stop both tee and engine recording paths; verify file name/output and scheduled-recording confirmation.
11. **Sleep, persistence and exit (p3, p5, p16, p17).** Check sleep countdown and end-of-episode behavior. Verify saved resume positions on exit, stop and sleep, then restore on next launch. Verify Trakt/Simkl progress after watching. Repeat live Home/resume and finish/exit while a delayed operation is outstanding; observe cancellation/parking without stale UI.
12. **Close the session.** Record every outcome and any unsupported/pending scenario, final CI/decoder status and artifact provenance. Keep device or decoder work outstanding until actual evidence exists. Attach reproducible regressions as follow-ups; this structural series does not authorize fixes.
