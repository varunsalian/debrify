## Mutable collections and borrowed aliases

`torrents` and `webDownloads`: fetch clears/refills in place; successful single/all/bulk delete removes in place. Search reset clears `allTorrentsForSearch`, and search fetch replaces only where the original assigns. `selectedTorrentIds` / `selectedWebDownloadIds`: insertion-order sets; toggles add/remove, select-all clears/refills, successful bulk clears both, cancellation removes completed IDs only. `navigationStack`: add/remove/clear of original records; paths and view nodes are reassigned only at existing statements. `torrentViewModes` / `webDownloadViewModes`: original map putIfAbsent/index writes; getters remain live. Sorting works on original local copies; series virtual folders keep their original children and ordering. The active selected set, current files, current items and filtered search getters must stay live (no snapshots in wiring). Download-all temporarily aliases/replaces currentTorrent then restores it after its dialog. Every listener borrows the original controller or FocusNode identity.

## Cross-section and wiring audit

Every method reference in the inventory goes to its named group in the manifest. Method callbacks are typed from the original signature; getter callbacks invoke the destination getter only at the original use. Widget values are live zero-argument callbacks. Fetch callbacks preserve reset named arguments and distinguish torrents from web downloads. Listener add/remove pairs use the same catalogLoading.onScroll / onWebDownloadScroll tear-offs; back registration/unregistration uses the same backNavigation.handleBackNavigation tear-off. TV focus callbacks retain their original closure. Deferred closures into later steps preserve original call-time evaluation, with final destinations fixed by the manifest (steps 4–6); no TEMP remains at completion. Scaffolds are activated and manually reviewed against these bindings before pinning.
