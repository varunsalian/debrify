## Mutable collections and borrowed aliases

Torrent/download lists clear and refill in place on reset; pagination appends in API order. Single/selected deletion removes successful ids in place. Delete-all re-fetches unfiltered data (including hidden undownloaded torrents), clears only the list on completion and refreshes on cancellation; selections are untouched. Both selection sets retain insertion order; completion clears both sets/leaves selection; cancellation removes only completed active ids and retains mode. folderPath removeLast/clear stays outside setState at its original position. Navigation/tree/view nodes replace only at original assignments; putIfAbsent stays outside setState. Search results/allTorrentsForSearch replace only as written; derived getters stay live. The ten focus nodes, two scroll controllers, four text controllers and handoff are borrowed with their original initialization and disposal ownership. Late final first-item and torrent-search nodes are assigned only in initState. No aggregated disposal. Sorting copies the original lists; recursive nodes keep identity.
