How these behave on the box

Every ident here obeys the invariants the shipped seven proved out on the Mi Box: the static full-screen ground lives in ident.backdrop (rastered once, outside the painter's RepaintBoundary), the painter draws only what moves, and at progress 1.0 the frame is completely motionless — noise is progress-derived, wobble and sparks damp to zero — because the settled splash is held while Home prepaints behind it.

Per-ident cost notes

  • Aperture, Swiss, Origami, Blueprint — flat fills, hairlines and clip-band reveals only. Cheapest of the round; no masks at all.
  • Ripple — the reflection is one cached Picture drawn through N sliced transforms; slice offset damps to 0, so the settled frame is a plain second draw.
  • Ember, Anamorphic — colour-changing text uses pre-baked quantized sets (the IdentAlphaSets idiom widened to colour), never a per-frame style mutation.
  • Silk — 30 flat vertical bands per frame plus a dashed stitch stroke via PathMetrics; the fold phase eases to a constant, so at rest it is a static band field.
  • Rack Focus — bokeh is drawn as ring + fill circles, halved on TV; no blur filter anywhere, the defocus reads from radius and rim alone.
  • Constellation — stars batch through drawRawPoints over a reused Float32List, twinkle amplitude damps to zero at rest.

Loading sweep adopts each ident's accent (the swatch on every card). The ~300 ms native window before Flutter's first frame stays a flat colour for all ten.
