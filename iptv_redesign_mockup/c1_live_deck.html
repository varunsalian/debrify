Settings in this concept

Settings stay close to today’s two-pane, but the rail order mirrors the deck: Sources → Recording → Lists → Startup. Each source row carries its counts and a one-tap “On-now shelves” toggle (which categories get a rail). The Recording pane gains the future Library row.

Why this concept: zero-thought browsing — the guide works for you. Channel-number die-hards and 50k-catalog spelunkers drop into the Guide overlay instead; the deck never tries to list everything.

Watch out: shelves need now/next for every visible channel — the per-row EPG budget you already manage (lazy fetch as rows bind) becomes the page’s heartbeat.
