Settings in this concept

Settings shrink: sources, lists, startup, and recording all became rail destinations ON the page, so the settings screen keeps only what’s configuration, not navigation — source CRUD and EPG URLs, the engine toggle, startup mode. The two-pane rail mirrors the page rail 1:1, so muscle memory transfers.

Why this concept: everything the app can do is visible from one screen; nothing regresses because every piece already ships. The stage-schedule-with-REC is the single highest-value addition.

Watch out: phone width can’t afford three zones — the sheet IS the stage there, and it must not fight the existing hold-OK list picker gesture.
