Concept 4 · Zapper

The page is the TV. Opening IPTV drops straight into the last channel playing full-bleed (your startup-channel feature, promoted to the default). Everything else is a translucent overlay: a channel strip on ▼, guide on OK, sources on ◀, number-pad zapping, one red record button. Zero chrome when idle — overlays fade out and it’s just television.

Settings in this concept

Nearly untouched — the Zapper is a MODE, not a reorganization. One new setting: “Open IPTV into: Zapper / Browse” (generalizing today’s startup-channel toggle). Sources, lists, recording all keep the current two-pane.

Why this concept: it makes Debrify feel like a television, not an app about television. Fastest possible time-to-picture; the DVR is one button while you watch.

Watch out: browse/VOD/series need a second surface anyway (the guide overlay), always-on playback costs bandwidth on metered phones, and a full-bleed preview under overlays is exactly the GPU tax your TV perf playbook warns about — the strip must be a cheap layer, and idle chrome must fully unmount.
