Recommendation — compose, don’t pick

These aren’t really competitors; they’re layers of one app:

Skeleton: C3 Command Center — evolve the shipped rail/list/stage into the cockpit (stage day-schedule with inline REC is the single highest-value, lowest-cost piece in all five mocks). Nothing regresses; ships incrementally behind the existing redesign flag.

Guide surface: C2 Grid — added as a view toggle on the page (and the player guide later). It is the DVR’s natural home and the grammar TV users already know.

Destinations: C5 Library + Scheduled — two rail entries in C3’s rail (phone: pushed pages). Library is the missing piece regardless of which design wins.

Modes: C4 Zapper is the startup-channel experience formalized (open-into-TV stays a setting), and C1’s On-Now shelves become the Watch/home treatment where discovery matters.

Suggested build order: ① stage schedule + inline REC (C3) → ② rail Library/Scheduled destinations (C5) → ③ grid guide view (C2) → ④ On-Now shelves (C1) → ⑤ Zapper polish (C4).
