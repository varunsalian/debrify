Why this reads premium. Zero opaque surfaces — the four panes (nav strip, source dock, browser, now-playing band) are one consistent material: 28px blur, 10% hairline, soft drop. The stage box is gone; focusing a channel retunes the entire backdrop (this is exactly the serial preview-player ladder that already ships — same player, new canvas). Focus is a white inner ring + brighter glass, no colour. The palette is stolen from the broadcast itself, so a football match makes the page green-gold and a film noir makes it silver — the UI recolours itself by content, which is what makes tvOS feel rich.

Feature carry. Sources + Favorites + custom lists live in the top dock (LEFT/RIGHT along it, counts inline; overflow scrolls); Live/Movies/Series tabs + category dropdown + submit-only search in the browse pane; every row keeps number, logo, now + progress, 4K/UHD badge, ★, REC state; RIGHT on a row opens the day schedule flyout with NOW/REC/REPLAY (catch-up); the now-playing band is the focused channel's full EPG line + Watch/Record/Favorite; the red dot top-right pulses while the engine records (jump-to-Recordings); "Updated" SWR chip in the browser footer; hold-OK list picker unchanged. Startup-channel mode is literally this page with the panes faded out. Phone: the browse pane becomes the page, the now-band becomes the bottom sheet.

Honest cost. On Android TV the preview plays on the native SurfaceView under Flutter (punched hole), so a BackdropFilter cannot actually sample the video — true glass blur is off the table there. That's fine: ship the panes as translucent tint + hairline (exactly what the Home hero's blend/tint stage already does over the same underlay, and what this mock's scrim is doing most of the work with), and reserve real blur for the texture path on phone/desktop. The look survives; the invariants (no Opacity/saveLayer over the hole, no fades on TV) are respected.
