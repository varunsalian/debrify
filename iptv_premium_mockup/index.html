IPTV, three ways it could feel expensive

Same Command Center skeleton the app ships today — rail → guide → stage, every feature carried — three different ideas of what "premium" means. Pick a voice, not just a layout.

What changed vs the old mocks (and the current page): no emoji glyphs (stroke SVG only), no blue→purple gradients, no gold focus outlines, no filled chips, no boxed panels. Each concept commits to one material, at most one accent colour with a fixed meaning, and one signature element the others don't have. All three keep the shipped DPAD grammar: LEFT-cascade to rail then app sidebar, hold-OK pickers, RIGHT-to-schedule, submit-only search, Offstage schedule swap, focus-node + row-extent contracts.
