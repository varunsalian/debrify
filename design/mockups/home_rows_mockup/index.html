Rows that say what they are

Four treatments of the same three fixes: headings become editorial and bright ("Popular Movies", not "Cinemeta: Popular"), the addon becomes a quiet tag instead of a shouted prefix, and catalog cards drop their redundant captions and grow into the freed space. The naming grammar applies everywhere including TV; the scale, caption and spacing changes are non-TV only. Concepts differ in exactly one thing — where the tag lives.

The grammar

Heading = catalog + content type. Tag = the addon, demoted to provenance. Rows that shared a name stop colliding.

Scope & wiring

Where the words come from

  • Kill the baked prefix: '${addon.name}: ${catalog.name}' at stremio_service.dart:1929 and search_screen.dart:2212 / 4550 / 5726.
  • Heading = catalog.name + _sectionTypeLabel() ("Popular" + "Movies"). Guard the double: if the catalog name already ends in the type word ("New Movies"), don't append it again.
  • Tag = addon.name. Tracker list rows already carry the right shape ("Watchlist · Trakt") — unchanged.

Device scope

  • All devices, TV included: the naming grammar (heading words + addon tag).
  • Non-TV only: heading scale/weight, caption removal, larger posters, 34px inter-row air. TV rails stay chrome-free and DPAD-paginated, exactly as today.
  • Applies to Spotlight and the classic _railHeader path alike — one grammar, two renderers.

Captions rule

  • Catalog cards: none — the art is the label, as in the reference.
  • Continue Watching: keep, but make it information ("48 min left"), not the title again.
  • Channel/logo rails (IPTV, Debrify TV): keep names — a logo tile without its name is a guess.
