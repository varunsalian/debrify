One quiet pill, three distances.

A 90-second countdown with live sync status, sized for a phone in hand, a laptop at arm's length, and a TV across the room. The same component everywhere — only the em scale and the safe-area inset change.

Demo time is compressed ≈7×: the real 90 s ladder plays in ~13 s. Attempts fire at the shipped rungs — 20 s, 45 s, 90 s — where the label briefly becomes “Checking”. Success ends the countdown immediately; it never waits for zero.
