The one big move

Only the focused avatar animates

Every tile holds a still frame; the focused one plays. A wall of looping GIFs would be noise on a phone and a decoder storm on a weak TV box. One moving thing is also how the eye finds focus from across a room — the animation IS the focus indicator, so it earns its cost.

Colour

The room takes the avatar's colour

A blurred bloom of the focused avatar washes the backdrop and tints its halo. Moving along the row repaints the whole screen, so a five-second decision feels designed rather than transactional.

Settings

The hub is a roster, not a control panel

Profiles becomes a top-level category — but it holds only the roster and the two genuinely device-wide choices. Everything about one person lives in that person's profile. Three rows here, not fourteen.

Getting pictures onto a TV

Three ways in

Built-in art, a file pick, or send-from-phone over the pairing channel that already exists. The last one matters most: nobody wants to type a URL or browse a filesystem with a D-pad.
