Concept 4 — Bar + Drawer

Classic Android in the original sense: a four-slot bottom bar for the daily core (Home · Discover · IPTV · Downloads) and a hamburger drawer carrying the complete sectioned list — the same sections the desktop rail uses. Nothing is ever more than the burger away, and the drawer doubles as a "you are here" map.

Why / why not

  • The complete map is always one gesture away, sectioned exactly like desktop — coherent cross-platform mental model.
  • Costs an app bar — today's phone pages are edge-to-edge with no top chrome; the burger needs a home on every tab, which is a visual tax on all of them.
  • Drawers are the dated pattern — Material moved away from them for primary nav; discoverability of edge-swipe is poor, and current-tab state inside the drawer is invisible until opened.
  • Build: low, but it reshapes every page's header — wider blast radius than it looks.
