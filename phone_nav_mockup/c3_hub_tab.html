Concept 3 — Hub Tab

Plex's answer to too-many-destinations: the fifth slot isn't a sheet, it's a page. The bar holds Home · Discover · IPTV · Hub · Settings, and the Hub is a proper browsable screen — sectioned cards with subtitles, live badges (a recording dot, download progress), room to grow. Deep destinations get a home instead of a popup.

Why / why not

  • Deep destinations get real estate — subtitles and live badges (active download, recording dot, "3 airing today") make the Hub a status page, not just a menu.
  • Grows forever — new sections cost a card, never a bar slot; gated items just drop their card.
  • Weakness: two of five slots go to meta-pages (Hub + Settings), so only three content tabs are one tap away — worse than C1/C2 for daily flows like Downloads.
  • Weakness: "where am I" — inside Debrify TV, the bar highlights Hub, one step less honest than C1's morphing slot.
  • Build: medium — the bar is trivial, but the Hub page is a real screen to design, badge-wire, and maintain.
