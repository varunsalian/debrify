Concept 2 — Your Five RECOMMENDED

Concept 1's shell, one upgrade: slots 2–4 belong to the user. Home anchors the left, More anchors the right, and the middle three are whatever this person actually lives in — the IPTV-first user below picked IPTV · Downloads · Debrify TV. Defaults are exactly C1, so nobody is forced to learn anything; the edit hides behind the More sheet for those who want it. This matches Debrify's existing DNA: nav is already per-user (hidden integrations, gated Calendar).

Why this wins

  • Day one it IS Concept 1 — classic defaults, no setup, Ezrik never needs to find the edit.
  • The five are never wrong for long — one drag fixes them, and gating (no debrid, no Trakt) self-heals the bar to defaults instead of leaving corpses.
  • House-consistent: Debrify already lets users hide integrations from nav; this is the same philosophy pointed at phones.
  • Build: C1 + a reorder sheet + one persisted list; the morphing More slot and gating rules are shared with C1 unchanged.
