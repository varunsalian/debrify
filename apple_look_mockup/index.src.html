Spotlight & Showcase

Two new TV layouts drawn at true panel scale — 1920×1080, which is 2× the app's logical pixels. Every number in the spec tables is the 1920-scale value; halve it for the Flutter constant. Artwork, titles, synopses, episode stills and cast photos are real, pulled live from Cinemeta and IMDb — the same two endpoints the app already uses.

Click a screen and drive it with the arrow keys. The focus model is as much the design as the paint is.

The focus effect is the point of this build. Apple's is five things at once — a 1.1 lift, a shadow that slides opposite the tilt, up to 10° of rotation on a 700px perspective, a 4pt shift, and a blurry white specular disc parked above the card's top edge. What no CSS easing can reproduce is the timing: damping 0.9 means every move overshoots and settles. Each value here is integrated by a real spring per frame, not tweened. Hit Focus: Apple / flat in the header to A/B it against what the app does today.

On tvOS the tilt tracks the remote's trackpad continuously. A d-pad has no pointer, so the honest analogue is to lean the card away from the direction focus travelled and let the spring carry it back through neutral — the 3D read comes out of the key press itself. Hover a focused card with the mouse to feel the trackpad version for comparison.

No credentials were read. Cinemeta and IMDb's GraphQL are both public and unauthenticated — the app stores no key for either. Simkl/Trakt would only add your watch state, which a design mock has no use for, so the token store was left alone.

Layout IIShowcase — Series

The backdrop holds the identity, then dissolves into an ambient colour field lifted off the same artwork as you descend, while the logo re-forms as a centred header. That dissolve is the single biggest reason Apple's page reads richer than ours — and we already own the engine (TvAmbientArtStage + dominant_color.dart).

Our own affordances are placed by what they are, not squeezed into Apple's row. DetailActionRow currently puts up to seven things in a Wrap — Play, Trailer, Sources, a source-count pill, More, and the Trakt and Simkl pills — against Apple's one pill and two circles. Shrinking seven to fit is how you get something that looks like the idiom and behaves like a toolbar. So: Trakt and Simkl leave the row and become marks in the meta line beside the rating, filled when tracked — tracker state describes what a title is to you, it is not an errand. And Sources becomes a band, which is the closest one-to-one mapping in the whole exercise: Apple ends its page with How to Watch, and that is literally our source list. Because sourceCount is boundSourceCount — bound sources, already local — that band paints with no network on open, and Bind stops being a mystery button in the identity row and becomes an action on the card it applies to. The row settles at four, the same four on every title.

The art-supply problem, made visible. Each hero below carries a measured left-third luminance. Apple's editorial stills keep a clean dark field on the left; metahub backdrops are frame grabs and often don't. Above roughly 0.32 the identity stack should flip to the right edge — the mock does this automatically, and you can watch it happen as you page. Metahub also ships some black wordmarks, which vanish on ink; the fetch measures logo luminance and drops anything under 0.30 to the text-title fallback.
