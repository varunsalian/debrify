Discover — layout option

Not Canvas ported over: Discover has no rails for Canvas's UP/DOWN rail switcher to move through, and it already owns Canvas's other half (the full-bleed trailer stage, the theater ladder, the chrome-dim relay). What's actually missing is the art-first browse posture. So: a Discover-only pref with two layouts — Grid (today, default) and Stage. Frames are 960×540, the real TV logical canvas.

AGrid — what ships today, for comparison

Two-pane: the reactive detail rail on the left, the quiet filter line and the poster grid on the right. ~8 titles legible at once. This stays the default — it's the right shape for scanning a catalog you don't know.

BStage — the proposed second layout

The rail dissolves: the focused title's backdrop goes full-bleed, its identity block (logo, meta, two lines of plot) sits bottom-left over the art, and the results become one shelf across the bottom. The filter line survives intact, moved to the top-left — UP from the shelf still lands on it, exactly as UP from the grid does today, so the source/type/catalog/genre path is unchanged.

The density argument, honestly: 7 posters visible against the grid's 8. Nearly free, because the grid was already spending 37.5% of the screen on a rail that the stage now draws at full size. What you lose is the second row's peripheral read; what you gain is a full-frame backdrop and a trailer with somewhere to play. The 12 / 312 counter is there to replace the sense-of-place the second row was giving you.

No new gesture to learn, and no UP/DOWN rail axis — that's the part of Canvas that doesn't exist here.

CStage, lights out — the theater ladder it already has

Nothing new is being built here: the trailer stage, the veils and the theater dwell are already wired on Discover. Grid can only dim around a poster wall; Stage is the layout those pieces were drawn for — the chrome recedes and the trailer owns all 960×540, the logo and the AMBIENT chip holding.

DConsidered and dropped — keep the rail, shrink the grid to one row

The cheapest version: leave the two-pane alone and swap only the grid for a big-poster row. It reads worse than either neighbour — 3 titles at a time against the grid's 8, and the art you paid for is stuck in a 360px column that already showed it. All cost, no posture change. If Stage isn't worth building, the answer is to keep the Grid, not to build this.

EThe pref — its own key, not shared with Home

A separate discover_layout alongside tv_home_style, on its own page under Discover in the settings IA. One pref governing both screens would promise a symmetry the two layouts can't keep — Home's Canvas has a rail axis, Discover's Stage has a filter line, and neither has the other's. TV-only, same as the Home picker.

What this costs

The stage, the trailer ladder, the theater dwell, the chrome-dim relay and the detail enrichment all exist — Stage rearranges them, it doesn't add an engine. The real work is that the shelf has to live inside the shared See-All grid widget, because Discover's five sources (Continue Watching, Trakt, Simkl, MDBList, addon catalogs) all render through it — so a shelf mode there is a shelf mode for every one of them, and every one needs its DPAD path re-checked. That's the honest price, and it's the reason to decide on the layout before anything is built.
